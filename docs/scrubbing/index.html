Animator & Timeline Scrubbing

Photo mode freezes the world — the scrubber lets you move it, one frame at a time. While photo mode is open, every Animator and every Timeline (PlayableDirector) in the scene is snapshotted and halted, and the Camera tab's SCRUB controls step any of them to an exact frame: catch the apex of a jump, the crack of an impact, the exact blink you want.

  • TARGET cycles the scrubbable things in the scene.
  • Scrub samples the target deterministically — the same slider value always lands on the same pose, even at timeScale = 0, forwards or backwards.
  • Physics is frozen for the session (rigidbodies go kinematic), so ragdolls and props hold still while you work.

What the freeze covers¶

Animators and directors are the scrubbable targets, but the session freeze holds more of the scene still: legacy Animation components, particle systems, trail renderers (existing points are held and emission is frozen), VFX Graph effects, Cloth simulation and NavMeshAgent movement. Those are frozen for the session and released on exit; they cannot be scrubbed. Animation events are suppressed while you scrub, so stepping through an event frame does not fire footstep sounds or gameplay callbacks.

What restores on exit¶

The scrubber undoes its own writes and nothing else:

  • Speeds and pause flags it set are restored: animator and legacy Animation speeds, director times, rigidbody kinematic flags and velocities, and the paused particle, trail and VFX state.
  • Animator update modes are never written, so they are never restored either; whatever your game set stays in force.
  • States replay only on targets you actually scrubbed. A target you never touched was only slowed, never re-posed, so it resumes from wherever it was instead of snapping back to its session-start state. In Unscaled pause mode this also means mid-session changes your still-running game made to an animator (a ragdoll toggle, a CrossFade) survive exit.

Limitations¶

  • Base layer only. Scrubbing samples an Animator's layer 0 state; upper layers keep their frozen pose and do not follow the slider.
  • Hand-built PlayableGraphs. Only PlayableDirector components are discovered. A PlayableGraph your own code creates and plays is neither frozen nor offered as a scrub target.
  • Timeline audio goes silent. Pausing a playing PlayableDirector also silences its Timeline AudioTrack output for the whole session, regardless of the AudioPauseMode setting. See Integrations.

From script: PhotoModeController.Instance.Scrubber exposes Targets and Scrub(target, normalizedTime).
