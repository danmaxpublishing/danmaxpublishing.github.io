Animator & Timeline Scrubbing

Photo mode freezes the world — the scrubber lets you move it, one frame at a time. While photo mode is open, every Animator and every Timeline (PlayableDirector) in the scene is snapshotted and halted, and the Camera tab's SCRUB controls step any of them to an exact frame: catch the apex of a jump, the crack of an impact, the exact blink you want.

  • TARGET cycles the scrubbable things in the scene.
  • Scrub samples the target deterministically — the same slider value always lands on the same pose, even at timeScale = 0, forwards or backwards.
  • Physics is frozen for the session (rigidbodies go kinematic), so ragdolls and props hold still while you work.

On exit everything restores exactly: animator speeds, update modes and states, director times, rigidbody flags and velocities — the same restore contract as the camera, audio and time scale.

From script: PhotoModeController.Instance.Scrubber exposes Targets and Scrub(target, normalizedTime).
